# Sleep

An Android app for moving a late sleep schedule earlier, one small step at a time.

- **Goal schedule:** set when you usually wake up, your goal wake-up time and how much sleep you need.
- **Nightly target:** each night you get a wake-up target and a bed-by time. The target moves 15–30 minutes earlier only after you hit it (woke up within 30 minutes of the target) enough times.
- **Logging:** slide from when you fell asleep to when you woke up, then tap OK.
- **Timeline chart (week / month):** shows *when* you slept against your target window, so drift is easy to spot.
- **Stats:** average sleep and wake times, regularity, and nights on target.
- **Reminders:** a wind-down reminder 1 hour before your target bedtime, and one at your wake-up target.
- All data stays on the phone.

The approach follows standard advice for shifting a delayed schedule: anchor the wake-up time, move in 15–30 minute steps, get morning light, keep weekends the same.

## APK
Every push to the `sleep-tracker` branch builds `sleep.apk` on GitHub Actions and attaches it to a release.
